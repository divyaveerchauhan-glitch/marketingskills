## Positioning

**Primary positioning:** "India's Full-Stack Sachet CMO for D2C and Startup Brands"

**Positioning statement:**
> India's full-stack sachet CMO for D2C and startup brands — custom formulation, powder blending, and sachet manufacturing under one WHO-GMP, FSSAI-certified roof. From 500 units.

**Why this wins (scored 24/30 vs competitors):**
- Full-stack = formulation → blending → filling → packaging → export docs, all in one facility
- Creates switching cost: once a brand's formula is developed with Sonfus, moving away is painful
- D2C/startup focus owns the fastest-growing, most underserved segment in Indian nutraceuticals
- "From 500 units" = operational proof of accessibility
- WHO-GMP and speed are table stakes everyone claims — not the hook

**What to NEVER lead with:** "quality", "WHO-GMP certified" as the primary claim, "fast turnaround" — all table stakes.
**Always lead with:** Full-stack, D2C-accessible, sachet specialist, 500 units.

### STRATEGY

#### Brand Strategy
1. Define: what Sonfus stands for in the market (full-stack, D2C-accessible, sachet-specialist)
2. Articulate the brand promise: "You'll get a finished sachet product — formula, fill, packaging, docs — from one partner, from 500 units."
3. Map brand pillars: Expertise (sachet-only), Accessibility (500 units), Reliability (WHO-GMP, CoA), Partnership (full-stack)
4. Brand narrative arc: Indian nutraceutical market is booming → D2C brands are the fastest-growing segment → they can't find a CMO that works at their scale → Sonfus is built for exactly this

#### Go-to-Market Strategy
Produce: target segment priority, channel mix, messaging per segment, 90-day launch plan
- Segment priority: D2C startups (highest urgency, fastest to convert) → established brands expanding → international buyers
- Channels: Cold email (via Lead Engine), LinkedIn (founder thought leadership), WhatsApp (Indian buyers), trade shows (Vitafoods India, NutraceuticalBusiness), industry directories

#### Pricing Strategy
- Structure: per-batch fee + per-unit cost, MOQ tiers (500 / 2,000 / 5,000 / 10,000+)
- Positioning: price premium justified by full-stack (no separate formulator cost), low MOQ flexibility, certification value
- Competitive anchor: position slightly above Fermentislife/Brukem (manufacturing-only) and well below Akums/Biovencer (large-scale)

#### Market Sizing
- Indian nutraceutical market: USD 15.4–18B (2025), 12–15% CAGR
- D2C nutraceuticals: fastest-growing sub-segment, no dominant CMO partner owns the space
- Global addressable: supplement brands worldwide sourcing from India
- SAM: Indian D2C supplement brands + international brands importing from India
- SOM: Sachet/powder-specific brands with MOQ needs of 500–20,000 units

#### OKRs / Marketing KPIs
Produce quarterly OKRs with: objective, 3 key results, owner, measurement method
Core metrics to track:
- Leads discovered (Lead Engine), qualified leads, outreach sent, reply rate, quote requests, clients acquired
- Content: organic traffic, LinkedIn impressions, email open rate, WhatsApp reply rate
- Brand: share of voice in "sachet CMO India" search, inbound inquiry volume
