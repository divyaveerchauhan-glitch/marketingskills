## Target Buyers

### 1. D2C Supplement Brand (primary)
- Sells on Amazon, DTC website, or subscription boxes
- Has a product concept, no manufacturing capability
- Pain: CMOs want 10,000+ units, they need 500–2,000 to test
- What they need: Low MOQ, fast sampling, retail-ready packaging, compliance docs
- Message: "Launch your sachet brand without factory risk. Start from 500 units."

### 2. Supplement Startup (primary)
- Pre-revenue or early stage, building first product
- May not know formulation requirements
- Pain: No pharma background, don't know where to start
- What they need: Full-stack guidance from formula to finished product
- Message: "From idea to finished sachets. We handle formulation, manufacturing, and compliance."

### 3. Established Brand Expanding into Sachets
- Already sells tablets, liquids, or other formats
- Wants to add sachet SKUs without new supplier complexity
- Pain: Current CMO doesn't do sachets, or requires too high MOQ for a new format test
- Message: "Add sachets to your line without adding a vendor."

### 4. International Brand Sourcing from India
- Wants cost advantage of Indian manufacturing
- Concerned about quality and export compliance
- Pain: Finding a reliable Indian CMO that meets international standards
- Message: "India-manufactured, globally compliant. We've shipped to 10+ countries."

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Direct. Technically credible. No fluff.
**Style:** Short sentences. Specific claims only (no "high quality", always say what makes it high quality). Numbers wherever possible (500 units, 3 weeks, 10+ countries).
**Personality:** Founder-led. Warm but no-nonsense. Confident without being salesy.
**Avoid:** "passionate about quality", "excellence", "world-class", "seamless", "holistic", "end-to-end solutions" (ironic — we do offer end-to-end but the phrase is overused). Generic pharma language.
**Use:** Specific product names (protein sachet, electrolyte powder), specific formats (stick pack, pillow pack), specific numbers, specific countries, specific certification bodies (FSSAI, WHO-GMP not just "certified").
